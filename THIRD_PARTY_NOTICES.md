[Allan-Cao/lol-voice-lines](https://github.com/Allan-Cao/lol-voice-lines), pinned to
commit `85f70fb891f2471eda8a7a773479106b0d3bf88f`. The repository describes the
dataset as cleaned data scraped from the League of Legends Wiki.

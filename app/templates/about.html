01 / Source

A pinned historical snapshot

Rift Index uses the machine-friendly Allan-Cao/lol-voice-lines dataset at one immutable commit. The source was cleaned from League of Legends Wiki transcripts and is verified by checksum before indexing.

This is a historical snapshot, not a live catalog of every champion released since 2022.

02 / Search

Meaning, not keyword matching

Each transcript becomes a local vector using all-MiniLM-L6-v2. Chroma compares your query to those vectors with cosine distance, so related ideas can surface even without an exact word match.

The displayed percentage is a similarity ranking signal. It is not model confidence.
